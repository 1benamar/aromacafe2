# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step, self-hosted fonts and images, like the first Aroma Café web and the rest of the user's sites (inherited practice, not re-asked). Published through GitHub Pages; the new web replaces the previous one (repo `1benamar/aromacafe`) once the user approves it. The previous web stays saved locally in `webs/aroma-cafe-blanes`.

## Users

People walking the centre of Blanes, many of them visitors (French and other foreigners in season) and also locals, looking at their phone in the street or on Google Maps while deciding where to have breakfast, a coffee, tapas or a quick lunch. They want to see in seconds what the place looks like, what it serves and at what price, whether it is open now and how to get there.

## Product Purpose

The web of Aroma Café Blanes, a bar-cafetería with terrace at Passeig de Dintre, 4, 17300 Blanes (Girona). It has to make people want to come in: real photographs of the renovated interior and the terrace, the full carta with current prices, the opening hours with the state of today, and two clear actions: call to reserve and get directions. Success is a visitor who arrives at the terrace, or calls, after looking at the page.

## Positioning

A café on the Passeig de Dintre, the pedestrian street one block from the beach, with a terrace under the trees and an interior renewed in 2026: illuminated golden wave bar, white marble counter and tables, bottle-green velvet chairs, tropical leaf wallpaper, copper beer tap and a neon «Café» at the door. Breakfast until 12:00, toasts, tapas, salads, combo plates and sandwiches, coffee (Segafredo) and drinks, all on a printed bilingual carta (Catalan and Spanish) with fair prices.

## Operating Context

- Phone: 972 74 66 43 (Google). Mobile on the back of the carta: 617 800 358. WhatsApp on that mobile is not confirmed: do not offer WhatsApp.

- Instagram: @aroma_cafe_blanes (407 followers). Writes in Catalan.
- Services (Google and previous research): terrace, eat in and take away, no delivery, reservations by phone, pets allowed, suitable for children and groups, wheelchair accessible entrance, toilet and seating, card and mobile payment.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The carta and its prices come from photographs of the printed carta sent on 29 October 2026 (`_fuentes/carta.json`). Breakfast is served until 12:00. Prices include VAT. Sandwiches come in two sizes (big and small bread) where the carta gives two prices.
- Never invent dishes, prices, services, dates of foundation, awards or reviews. Every option a visitor can pick must exist on the carta or in this file.
- Web only in Spanish. Dish names translated from the carta's own Spanish lines.
- No online booking system: reservations by phone.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Aroma Café (Google writes «Aroma café Blanes»). The façade sign and the green awning write AROMA CAFÉ in classical Roman capitals; the carta cover writes «Aroma Café» in a brush script inside a gold cartouche with «BAR CAFETERÍA» below. No digital logo file exists: any wordmark is a reconstruction from the sign and must be flagged to the user.
- Real colours of the place: bottle-green awning, fascia and chairs; golden illuminated bar; white marble; black marble cover with gold frame on the carta; deep green carta pages.
- The user wants it formal, sober and simple, with photography carrying the visual weight, symmetric and well structured, and it must not read as AI-made (see memories). References the user likes: martinasweetcakes.com and 1benamar.github.io/Diamanda.

## Evidence on Hand

- Previous research in `webs/aroma-cafe-blanes/assets/photos/source/`: 25 Google photos (g18 to g22 are other cafés: never use) and 15 Instagram images (mostly small).
- Real reviews exist on Google but none were collected verbatim: do not show quotes unless they are copied from Google with author.

## Product Principles

1. The place sells itself: show the real interior, terrace and food, large and well lit.
2. Useful first: carta with prices, today's hours and how to get there within one scroll on a phone.
3. Truth over decoration: nothing on the page the café does not actually do.
4. Calm and formal: elegant like the renewed interior, never loud.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard access, reduced motion respected, readable in direct sunlight on a phone.
